# 10. How We Answer "Why Not NoBroker, Nestaway, CBRE, JLL, or Build It Ourselves?"

> "That's a fair question. We don't see NoBroker, Nestaway, CBRE, or JLL as exact substitutes because their primary models and customer segments are different. Our focus is to become the post-handover operating partner for residential assets. We want to work with developers and owners across the lifecycle of an asset—leasing, maintenance, owner support, resident operations, vendor coordination, and asset performance—not simply provide a single rental or facility service."

### If Rajapushpa says: "Why can't we build this ourselves?"

> "You absolutely could. The question is whether it makes strategic sense. Building a professional operations business requires leasing teams, maintenance coordination, customer support, inspections, vendor networks, compliance, technology, and ongoing service operations. Those are different capabilities from acquiring land, developing projects, and selling homes. Our goal is to specialize in post-handover operations so your team can continue focusing on what it does best."

### Strong differentiator

> **We don't measure success by how many properties we manage. We measure success by how well each property performs.**

### Questions
- How many completed projects do you currently operate?
- How do you support investor buyers after possession?
- What percentage of buyers purchase for investment?
- How many buyers are NRIs?

- How do you currently handle vacant investor-owned apartments?

- Do owners ask for property-management recommendations?

The first meeting is for understanding their workflow.
